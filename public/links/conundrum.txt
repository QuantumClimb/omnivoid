WELCOME TO THE OMNIVOID.

A new chapter dawns with Omnivoid Labs molded in design-thinking, sensory-perceptive, uncompromising original music experience, immersive visual art, coding, film and tech alongside education and information exchange via workshops, labs and at the center of it all, ebbing and reverberating with visceral, dissonant, sonic performances at a venue that believes in the true form of art. A captivating confluence of humankind and technology with an artist-first approach. Based out of Chennai, we are a collective of artists, poets, researchers, design thinkers, music producers and educators that form Omnivoid Labs.
